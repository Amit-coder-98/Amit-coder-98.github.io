THE PURPOSE

Useful examples,
with the work shown.

The channel covers everyday money concepts and practical AI tools for beginners. Its examples use fictional sample data, original graphics, and clear explanations of what was actually demonstrated.

The app supports the creator’s publishing work. The current workflow has produced a pilot, uploaded it privately, set its thumbnail, and checked its processing status. Ongoing unattended scheduling is still being configured.

This website explains the application and its data practices. Google authorization happens separately through the owner’s installed application.

THE WORKFLOW

From a useful question
to a checked upload.

  1. 01

    Prepare the example

    Develop a script and supporting visuals around a specific task. Keep fictional examples and actual tool results clearly identified.

  2. 02

    Check the video

    Inspect narration, captions, calculations, and the finished media. Hold an upload if required checks fail.

  3. 03

    Upload with permission

    Use Google authorization for the intended channel. Send the video, metadata, thumbnail, and applicable synthetic-content disclosure.

  4. 04

    Review performance

    Read authorized YouTube Analytics reports, such as views and watch time, to inform future editorial decisions.

GOOGLE ACCOUNT ACCESS

The channel owner
stays in control.

The application uses YouTube API Services to identify the authorized channel, upload content, and retrieve permitted channel information and Analytics reports.

Google sign-in runs on Google’s authorization pages. The app receives permission tokens rather than a Google password. The owner can revoke access through their Google Account.
