MONEY AI LAB AUTOMATION

Privacy policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

This policy explains how the personal publishing application uses information connected to the Money & AI Lab YouTube channel, and what happens when someone visits this informational website.

1. Who operates the application

Money AI Lab Automation is operated by the owner of the Money & AI Lab channel. The installed publishing application is currently for that owner’s use. This website provides app information, privacy disclosures, and terms.

Contact: limboleamit1815@gmail.com.

2. Information the application uses

The application uses YouTube API Services. Depending on the operation the owner authorizes, it processes:

  • Channel identity, including channel ID, name, and handle, to confirm the intended upload destination.
  • Videos and thumbnail files supplied by the owner; titles, descriptions, tags, language, audience, visibility, and disclosure settings.
  • Upload-session details, video IDs, processing results, and thumbnail information needed to finish or resume the same upload.
  • Authorized channel Analytics reports, such as daily views and watch time, for editorial review.
  • Google OAuth access and refresh tokens and granted permissions, to make authorized requests without collecting the owner’s Google password.
  • Operational records needed to diagnose a failed task and avoid duplicate uploads.

The website itself has no Google sign-in form, file-upload form, or account-registration form.

3. Google permissions and their purpose

The exact requested permissions are shown on Google’s consent screen. The current app does not request Gmail, Google Drive, financial-account, or YouTube monetary-report access.

4. How information is used and shared

Information is used to carry out the owner’s requested publishing tasks, verify the destination channel, recover an interrupted upload, and review performance. Private channel reports are not published on this website.

Google and YouTube receive the requests and content needed to operate their services. See the Google Privacy Policy. Media-generation providers selected by the owner may separately process scripts and media prompts; the current workflow does not send Google OAuth tokens or private YouTube Analytics reports to media-generation services.

When the owner uses Codex to run or inspect the workflow, code, content, and report summaries brought into that service can be processed by OpenAI under the owner’s account settings and OpenAI’s policies.

The app does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising profiles, credit decisions, or training its own general-purpose AI models. Google API data is used for the channel-management features described here. Any new use or new sharing of Google user data requires an updated disclosure and the owner’s consent before it begins.

5. Storage, security, and retention

In the current installed version, OAuth tokens and publishing records are stored on the operator’s device. Saved OAuth tokens are protected with Windows Data Protection for that Windows user. Other job metadata is stored in local files. The public website repository contains only the website’s public source and assets.

Retention is managed by the operator. Non-statistical YouTube API records must be refreshed or removed within 30 days. Authorized performance history may be retained longer where permitted, with authorization and the availability of the associated YouTube data rechecked at least every 30 days. Data that is no longer needed for its authorized purpose is removed.

Original media and scripts created or supplied by the owner are separate from retrieved YouTube API data. A move to another execution host or a material change in data processing requires this policy and the owner’s consent to be updated as needed.

6. Revoke access or request deletion

The owner can stop new authorized requests by revoking the app’s access in Google Account third-party connections. The app stops account-dependent tasks when authorization is no longer valid.

To request removal of stored app data, email limboleamit1815@gmail.com with the subject “Delete Money AI Lab Automation data” and identify the affected channel. The operator removes the associated stored tokens and retrieved channel data as soon as possible, within seven calendar days of the request.

Revoking authorization or deleting the app’s local records does not remove videos already uploaded to YouTube. Manage those videos directly in YouTube Studio. Google and YouTube retain information according to their own policies.

7. Website hosting and browser information

This static information site is prepared for GitHub Pages hosting. GitHub processes web-request information, including IP addresses, for hosting and security purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

This site does not add analytics scripts, advertising tags, tracking cookies, or external font requests. Following a link to YouTube, Google, or another provider opens a service governed by that provider’s policies.

8. Questions and changes

For questions or complaints about these data practices, contact limboleamit1815@gmail.com. The effective date is updated when this policy changes. Material changes to how Google user data is accessed, used, or shared are disclosed to the owner for renewed consent before those changes take effect.
